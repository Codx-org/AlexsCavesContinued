# 1.1.1

A small bug-fix release.

## Fixed

- **The Cave Compendium forgot everything you had unlocked after leaving and rejoining a world** when
  Alex's Mobs, Citadel or another Citadel-based mod was also installed. Alex's Caves now saves its
  progress under a name of its own; progress saved before this update is still picked up.
- **The Candicorn would not sprint or charge its ability** on Minecraft 1.21.2 and newer. The same
  fix covers the Subterranodon, Tremorsaurus, Tremorzilla and Atlatitan wherever they depended on how
  their rider steers.
- **Trilocaris drowned underwater** on Minecraft 1.20.2 and newer. It is amphibious again.
